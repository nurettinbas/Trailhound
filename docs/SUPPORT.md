# Support

**Email:** [nurettin.bas@icloud.com](mailto:nurettin.bas@icloud.com)

Use this address for app issues, general feedback, and feature requests.

## In the app

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Under **About**, tap **Report a problem**.
3. Read what the diagnostic attachment contains.
4. Choose **Open mail with log** (or the share sheet if Apple Mail is not set up).
5. Describe what went wrong. You can remove the attachment before sending.

The log does not include GPS coordinates, addresses, place/vehicle names, photos, or your notes. On-device logs are kept for at most 30 days.

If Mail is not configured, Trailhound copies `nurettin.bas@icloud.com` to the clipboard and offers the system share sheet so you can send `trailhound-debug.txt` from Gmail or another app.
