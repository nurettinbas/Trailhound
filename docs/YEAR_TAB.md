# Recap tab

Product layout for Trailhound’s **Recap** tab (Trips → Vehicles → Statistics → **Recap** → Settings). Same glass card language as Stats (`statsFullCard`, `StatsCard.swift`). Chrome follows the selected **shell palette**. Recap, badges, frequent routes, month forecast, and year awards live here — not on Statistics.

Recap does **not** run `StatsSnapshotLoader`. Recap / forecast / badges start on tab appear. Year awards is first and loads on appear (rollups + expenses only). Forecast is unfiltered (all vehicles), matching recap.

## Card language

Same chrome as [Stats](STATS_TAB.md):

| Span | Use |
|---|---|
| **Full** | Year recap, badges, frequent routes, month forecast, year awards |
| **Nested panel** | Variable-height frost in forecast expand (`.statsNestedPanel()`) — never a grouped `List` plate |

Year recap and month-forecast posters use `statsFullCard(contentInset: 0)` plus `.statsPosterOverlayPadding()` so artwork fills the card. Do not invent Recap-tab-only chrome.

## Cards

| Card | Role |
|---|---|
| **Year awards** | First card. Unfiltered calendar-year medal grid (distance, night owl, goal months, busiest day, costliest vehicle / month). Year picker when more than one year has trips. Recap is the story; awards are the grid. Locked medals use the same full-color disc + dim scrim under the glyph + rim lock badge as Badges — not a washed-out fill. Loads on appear (rollups + expenses only). |
| **Year recap** | Last card on the Recap tab. Compact poster: Canvas scene fills the card (`RecapHubTeaserScene`, `statsFullCard(contentInset: 0)`). Year frost chip top-left, km + Play on one row, trips caption below — same overlay padding as forecast. Idle teaser motion is 8 fps only while the row is on-screen, the scene is active, and Reduce Motion / Low Power / UI tests are off. Whole card opens the story. Full-screen cinematic story: palette Canvas fills the cover with **looping** motion (road dashes, needle sway, window twinkle). Pages **push** like Instagram (tap left ~⅓ of the **full screen including edges** to go back, tap the rest to go forward — no Next). Segment bars are full-width and sit **tucked under the Dynamic Island / status bar** like Instagram (window top + 8 pt; chrome ignores SwiftUI’s top inset so it is not double-spaced); Close and Share are 44pt Liquid Glass circles (`GlassNavCircleIcon` / `.glassCircleChrome()`) under them on the trailing edge and stay tappable. Overlay copy settles up. The badges page sits the 3D medals **inside the glowing orbs** (shared layout so Canvas halo and medal share a frame; 3+2 for five, second row centered, no overlap). Story pages add Canvas sparkles (stronger around each orb). Unlocks from that year first, otherwise your current medals. The last page is a year wrap — receding road + brand — not a slogan. Its segment fills like the others, then the story dismisses (tap forward also closes). Share captures the **visible page** (frozen scene + copy, 9:16), not a leftover km poster. Reduce Motion, hold-to-pause, background, and UI tests freeze the story. January uses the previous calendar year; other months are YTD. Corridor counts are year-scoped. Estimated driving fuel and logged expenses are separate lines, never summed. Cover is frozen at open. Autoplay once in December/January when the Recap tab appears. Unfiltered year, independent of Stats chips. Empty years show a static teaser + copy — no Play, no idle motion. |
| **Badges** | Horizontal strip shows **unlocked medals only** (centered when they fit, scroll if they overflow). Locked tiers stay in the gallery, **unlocked first** then catalog order. The compact card uses a plain `GlassToolbarSymbol` (glyph only). After expand, collapse is `GlassToolbarCollapseButton` (44pt Liquid Glass circle). That control grows the card into the gallery. Gallery cells are compact equal-size **glass** cards (primary/secondary glass ink, not faint hierarchical white). Compact 2 columns, Regular 3, accessibility Dynamic Type 1 column. Medals use 3D chrome (`AchievementMedalChrome`); km is silver / gold / **teal platinum** / **lavender diamond** (100 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 km) with **white** glyphs like every other family and the four km medals stay listed even before earlier km unlocks; other families each have a unique hue, icon, and idle (no shared pulse), including trips / hours / long haul / dawn / weekend / fleet / countries plus longer business, streak, city, night, and corridor rungs. Locked medals keep the full chrome; the family glyph stays opaque on the disc and the lock is a small rim badge (`AchievementMedalLockOverlay`), not a film over the icon; progress uses km vs counts. Unlocked first-trip flag waves from the pole (`AchievementFlagWaveEffect`) — not a pulse of the disc. Distance medals run the two path nodes along the S (`AchievementDistancePathGlyph`) with a different choreography per km metal (convoy / rendezvous / patrol / bloom); clock poses snap on a fixed side (List insert must not interpolate the nodes). Night medals bob the moon and stars together and sparkle (`AchievementNightSkyGlyph`). Compact strip and gallery idle is `achievementIdleClock` (12 fps `CADisplayLink`; tab swap is scoped; an unscoped TabView `animation = nil` would freeze it). Gallery idle starts after the card-grow morph so motion does not cancel the spring. The 100 km one-trip car stays on the disc and rides a hill (nose pitch; never fades). Share is unlocked-only: a compact palette-tint chip (`.trailhoundCompactProminentButton()`, same recipe as recap Play) exports a 9:16 PNG (`AchievementShareRenderer` — atmosphere, Trailhound mark, frozen glass plate, 3D medal + copy) for Instagram / WhatsApp stories or Messages, not a title string. Reduce Motion skips the morph and idle glyph motion. Newly unlocked badges play a glass overlay (family medal motion, 3 s each, then the next unseen badge). Already-earned medals play that overlay once after the catalog backfill (no inbox), so missed unlocks can be watched on Recap. A Recap refresh appends new unlocks; it does not reset the card on screen. Tap skips to the next. |
| **Frequent routes** | Top corridor + MapKit snapshot thumbnail framed on that corridor. Same compact glyph / expanded circle as Badges. Expand is the **map itself**: a full-screen MapKit view is scaled into the list card, then unsqueezes to fill the screen — the map host does not resize, so tiles do not pan up. Preview chrome fades on the same spring; collapse is `GlassToolbarCollapseButton` (44pt Liquid Glass circle) and the caption fades in. Camera fits the visible habit set (clamped to ~70 km). Habit portrait: repeated corridors (`count ≥ 2`) in the same metro (max 8), **driving polylines**. Tap another line to focus. Corridors merge by saved place or ~500 m geo cell; A→B is B→A. Privacy-safe home/work labels. |
| **Month cost forecast** | Projected month-end total. Driving fuel estimate + installments + other expenses; **logged pump fuel is a separate line** and is never double-counted into the hero total. Hub is a Recap-style **poster**: Canvas sparkline fills the card edge-to-edge (`StatsForecastSparklineScene`, `statsFullCard(contentInset: 0)`). Layout is a full-bleed poster — title frost chip top-left, huge amount + caption metrics bottom-left, artwork breathing in the middle. Copy sits on a bottom atmosphere gradient (no whitish veil). The sparkline is a glow stroke + area in the complementary hue of the selected shell (`forecastSparklineStroke` / `ShellRGB.complementaryInk` — Gold reads blue, Sky reads warm; never a fixed orange). The “higher / lower this month” line uses that same ink; confidence stays secondary caption. The 6 pt mix bar stays in expand only. Same compact expand glyph as Badges / Frequent routes (top-trailing). Expand matches Badges: `AtmosphericBackground` + frozen `glassCard`, not a clear sheet over Recap. The card grows into a taller sparkline poster (`posterExpandedHeight`), composition bar + `StatsSegmentSwatch` legend, and frost breakdown rows (`.statsNestedPanel()`), not a grouped `List`. Collapse is `GlassToolbarCollapseButton` (44pt Liquid Glass circle). Independent of the Stats comparison **Logged vehicle expenses** card. Motion is one-shot (number, trend settle, entrance glint); no `TimelineView` on the Recap list. Reduce Motion and UI tests freeze it. |

**Year awards** is first on the tab. **Year recap** is last. Recap is the story; awards are the medal grid.

Deep links `trailhound://stats/forecast|recap|routes|achievements` open this tab and the matching expand/cover (URLs stay under `stats/` so widgets and inbox rows keep working). The Home Screen **monthly cost forecast** widget uses `trailhound://stats/forecast`. `trailhound://stats/goal` stays on Statistics. A badge unlock writes an inbox row and a local banner (skipped if Recap is already selected — the overlay is enough). Medals already earned from trips that existed before badges shipped are replayed on launch (and again after new badge families ship): they show as unlocked, without inbox or banner. Recap plays the unlock overlay once for those already-earned medals so missed celebrations can be watched; later visits stay quiet. The gallery also backfills trip-count and related medals from completed trips, so a 50-trip medal opens when you have 50 trips. In January, a local notification plus inbox row opens last year’s recap when that year has trips and `recap.seen.{year}` is unset (1 Jan 09:00, with catch-up if the app opens later in January). Watching the story or December–January autoplay marks it seen so the push does not repeat.
